Responsible AI
This entire application runs its AI inference on this device, with explicit guardrails on what the model is allowed to consider.
🔐 On-device architecture
AI inferenceLOCAL
Résumés uploaded externally0
External AI API calls0

AI modelLocal (Phi Silica)

Résumé text extraction (PdfPig), candidate profile/fit reasoning, interview question generation, duplicate-application search (on-device semantic index), and the Recruiting Copilot all run through Microsoft.Windows.AI.* APIs directly on this PC's NPU — no résumé, candidate name, or assessment ever leaves the device. *Internet is not required for inference once the on-device model is provisioned.

⚖ Fairness guardrail

Every fit-assessment prompt sent to the model includes this explicit instruction, verbatim:

"Base your evaluation ONLY on job-relevant qualifications: skills, experience, education, and certifications found in the résumé text. Do NOT factor in the candidate's name, gender, age, ethnicity, nationality, or any other characteristic not directly relevant to job qualifications — evaluate the work only."

This is a prompting guardrail, not a formal fairness guarantee — the recruiter should still review every assessment and evidence trail before making a decision. Every score is fully traceable back to the exact résumé text that produced it via "Show me the evidence."
